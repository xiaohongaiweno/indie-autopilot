# Conversion and analytics playbook

## Journey
Discovery → landing page → signup/download → first value → repeat use → paid conversion or recommendation.

## Review
- Does the first screen explain who the product is for and what problem it solves?
- Is the primary CTA clear and functional?
- Are pricing, limits, privacy, compatibility, and setup requirements clear?
- Is there a short path to experiencing the product's value?
- Are important events tracked accurately?
- Are analytics collected in a privacy-conscious way?

## Suggested scorecard
- Search impressions and clicks, with date range
- Non-brand queries and landing pages
- Referral sessions by source
- Directory listings live and qualified visits
- Content engagement and link clicks
- Signups/downloads/activation, if tracked
- Qualified user conversations and feedback
- Time spent per channel

## Interpretation
Separate leading indicators from business outcomes. Compare equivalent date ranges. Annotate major site/product changes. Avoid claiming causality from one post or link without supporting evidence. Do not invent missing metrics.
