# Case study: SnapByFace promotion workflow

## Purpose
Use this as an initial case study for learning how a completed product still needs ongoing promotion. It is not a universal template; apply only the tactics that fit the next product.

## Known promotion activities from the founder's experience
- SEO and Google indexing troubleshooting
- Topic/keyword cluster planning
- Product directory submissions and listing troubleshooting
- Free backlink and mention outreach
- Product Hunt launch follow-up
- AlternativeTo listing and alternatives setup
- Reddit community selection and sharing a demo video
- YouTube video publication
- WhatsApp sharing considerations
- Evaluating unsolicited SEO/backlink service offers and sample-placement claims
- Creating promotional assets such as logos and launch copy

## Lessons to encode
1. A launched product still needs a coordinated, persistent growth workflow.
2. Search indexing, on-page SEO, distribution, content, and user acquisition are distinct workstreams.
3. Platform rules and submission processes change; verify them at the time of use.
4. Free-first research should distinguish genuinely free submission from paid upsells.
5. Maintain evidence: a sent email, submitted form, live listing, live link, indexed URL, and referral traffic are different states.
6. Avoid trusting backlink sales pitches without examining actual placement URLs, relevance, link attributes, and risks.
7. Community promotion must follow each community's rules and offer value rather than only advertise.
8. Produce platform-specific English copy and step-by-step instructions to reduce execution friction.
9. Do not assume every channel or SEO tactic will fit a different product category.

## Unknowns
Do not invent historical results such as traffic lift, keyword rankings, signups, conversions, or which specific submissions caused growth. Ask the founder for data or mark outcomes as unknown.
