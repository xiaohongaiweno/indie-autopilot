# Relevant backlink and mention playbook

## Preferred opportunities
- Relevant resource pages
- Legitimate product directories
- Tutorials, reviews, and comparison articles where the product is genuinely useful
- Founder interviews, guest contributions, expert commentary
- Integration/partner directories
- Community answers where the link adds real value
- Original research or useful free resources that naturally earn citations

## Exclude or flag
- Paid links sold to manipulate rankings
- Bulk backlink packages and private blog networks
- Irrelevant directories with no audience fit
- Guaranteed ranking claims
- Sites with deceptive ownership or suspicious outbound link patterns
- Requests for fake reviews or undisclosed endorsements

## Evaluation fields
Prospect URL, owner/editor contact method, topic relevance, audience fit, reason for link, cost status, policy, effort, risk, outreach angle, evidence, status, next step.

## Outreach principles
- Personalize each message.
- State the honest reason for contact.
- Do not mass-spam or repeatedly follow up after refusal.
- Do not fabricate relationships, metrics, testimonials, or user outcomes.
- A link being live does not prove traffic or ranking impact.
