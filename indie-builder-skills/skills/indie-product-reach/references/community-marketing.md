# Community promotion playbook

## Research before posting
For each subreddit, forum, launch community, or Q&A site:
1. Confirm it is active and relevant to the audience.
2. Find and read current official rules.
3. Determine whether self-promotion or external links are allowed.
4. Identify suitable formats: question, build log, tutorial, case study, demo, feedback request.
5. Prepare an original post that gives value without requiring a click.
6. Disclose maker affiliation where appropriate.
7. Avoid duplicate cross-posting and repetitive promotional comments.

## Post package
- Community and official rules URL
- Audience fit and rationale
- Proposed title
- Post body
- Product disclosure
- Link policy
- Recommended posting timing only when evidence exists
- Replies to likely questions
- Publication checklist
- Metrics to record

## Measurement
Track live URL, date, impressions if available, comments, votes/reactions, qualified conversations, referral sessions, signups/downloads, moderation outcome.

Never fake votes, accounts, endorsements, or personal experience. Do not attempt to bypass moderation.
